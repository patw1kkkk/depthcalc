DepthCalc

An open-source toolkit for creating bathymetric lake maps from GPS + depth measurements.

Capture ↗Collect measurements on the lake.Studio ↗Turn your sessions into coverage and contour maps.

How it works

  1. Collect with Capture. Open it on your phone, allow GPS, and start a named session. Enter the depth from your fish finder and tap Add measurement at each location.
  2. Save your CSV. End the session to download your measurements. Keep the file as a backup and transfer it to the device you use for Studio. You can download it again in Capture.
  3. Create a map in Studio. Select your lake on the map, import your session CSV files, then generate a depth map. Adjust the chart settings and export your map.

Local first. No account. Your measurements stay on your device.
